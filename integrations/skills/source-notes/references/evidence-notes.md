# Evidence and writing

For video, retain a frame when it makes a diagram, code state, terminal result, physical setup, comparison, or explanatory transition easier to verify. Reject presenter-only and duplicate frames. Preserve candidate ID, timestamp, package-relative image, `review_mode`, and `approved_by`. An empty selection requires a concrete no-useful-visuals reason.

Write a self-contained Chinese note that rebuilds the source's explanation. Organize concepts in causal order, retain useful examples and caveats, and keep evidence locators close to the claims they support. Cover every knowledge block in `notes_input.md` or state why it was excluded. Use only package-local images and include every approved image.

Use the source's natural structure. Do not force mastery labels, a recap, misconceptions, or empty template sections when they do not help.
